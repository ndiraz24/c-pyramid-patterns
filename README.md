# c-pyramid-patterns

A collection of pyramid and pattern exercises implemented in C.

- **pyramids1.c**: Full pyramid of stars
- **pyramids2.c**: Half pyramid of stars
- **pyramids3.c**: Half pyramid of numbers
- **pyramids4.c**: Half pyramid of repeating alphabets
- **pyramids5.c**: Inverted half pyramid of stars
- **pyramids6.c**: Inverted half pyramid of numbers
- **pyramids7.c & pyramids8.c**: Full palindromic/symmetric number pyramid
